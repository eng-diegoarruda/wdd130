We’re here to give you a safe, exciting river trip you’ll talk about for years—while helping everyone respect and enjoy the outdoors.

History

White Water Rafting Co. started with a few local river guides, one raft, and a simple goal: share the rush of running wild rapids with others.

What began as a small crew has grown into a trusted adventure team known for top-notch safety, experienced guides, and fun days on the water.
